Detailed Description

Latest completed execution (e.g. an async read or write cycle) and its duration, published by one thread and consumed by another one without locking.

Each completed execution is consumed at most once, and a consumed duration always belongs to the execution it is reported for: the duration and its sequence number are published together through a sequence lock. Executions published between two consume_new() calls but the latest are skipped.

publish() may only be called from a single thread, and consume_new() and discard() from a single (possibly different) thread.
